Let’s Grow!!

Diversify Beyond Students Before Q2's Seasonal Dip

The Risk (77.3% Student Dependent)

Q2 (Apr-Jun) spans the end of spring semester. Q1 showed a ridership dip around mid-March spring break. A business that's 77.3% student-dependent will feel that seasonal swing directly in its Q2 numbers.

Recommendation

Launch a targeted campaign for local residents and leisure riders ahead of Q2 so the summer-semester lull doesn’t translate one-to-one into a rental slump.

Let’s Grow!!!

Demand Follows the Class Schedule

No sharp rush hour

Ridership climbs steadily from 7am and stays high from noon to 6pm — a single wide plateau, not a typical commuter bike-share shape.

Recommendation:

Shift bike-availability checks and promo pushes to the midday-afternoon (12-6pm) where the real volume lives.
