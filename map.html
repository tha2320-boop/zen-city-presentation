Data notes & methodology

16,578 trips (Q1 2022) are counted here, out of 16,585 raw trip records. Seven were excluded: six had implausible durations of 32–81 hours (a bike checked out and never properly docked, not a real point-to-point ride), and one was a one-off pop-up event ("Springfest 2022") with no real end station.

Station IDs 2498 and 3794 were swapped in the source export — every one of the 4,150 trips tagged id 2498 is consistently named "Dean Keeton/Speedway" in the raw data, and every one of the 33 trips tagged id 3794 is consistently named "4th/Sabine." Since station_info.xlsx says id 2498 is officially 4th/Sabine and id 3794 is Dean Keeton & Speedway, trusting the id alone would put 4,150 trips at a small downtown stop whose real neighbors see 60–1,000 trips a quarter. This map trusts the internally-consistent station names instead, which puts that volume at Dean Keeton & Speedway — consistent with every other top-10 station being on or next to UT Austin's campus.

Per-station totals cover only the 78 currently active stations. Six trips that ended at a since-closed station ("ACC – Rio Grande & 12th") are included in system-wide departure counts but don't appear on any dot here, since that station no longer exists.
